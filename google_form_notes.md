# Google Form — Broad Area of Study sub-category grid

Options for the per-area sub-category grid (Section 3, `preferences.specific_courses`). One question
per Broad Area; **a student only ever sees their own area's option list** — never the other seven. Any
wording that disambiguates two similar-sounding options must therefore be self-contained; a
cross-reference to an option in another column is invisible to them.

## Format rule (keep this — it's what makes the list skimmable)

`Title — 3-4 concrete nouns`, max ~5 words after the dash. No semicolons, no cross-references, and
the hook must never restate the title. Hooks name **subfields** by default, and the **career** instead
where the job is famous but the subfields aren't (Law, Medicine, Pharmacy, Nursing, Education).

Uniform hook length is the point: most students fill this on a phone, where a 13-word hook wraps to
three lines and a 4-word one doesn't. A ragged column costs full attention on every line, which is how
a busy student ends up abandoning the form.

Revised 2026-09-01 (twice). First pass cut jargon and true duplicates — genuinely distinct fields kept
separate but reworded around a relatable hook, merging only where fields truly overlap in practice
(Computing) or where an option was too niche to stand alone (Naval, folded into Mechanical). Second
pass applied the format rule above: filled five bald options (Chemistry and Physics had no description
at all), cut bloated hooks, removed hooks that restated their title, and replaced the broken
`see #24` cross-reference on Biomedical Science with self-contained wording.
