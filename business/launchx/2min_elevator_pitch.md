[0:00] Intro

Hi, I'm Evan from Shortlisted. Choosing a university is a four-year decision that can cost a Malaysian family hundreds of thousands of ringgit.

[0:10] Need

I surveyed 84 students. Most paid no one for help. They're juggling three questions at once: is this uni right for me, can I actually get in, and can my family afford it once you add rent and living costs, not just tuition?

[0:30] Approach

Shortlisted is an AI research agent. A student gives us their grades, budget, countries, course and the student life they want. The agent researches every university that fits and returns a ranked list, split into reach, match and safety. Every fee, entry requirement and deadline is checked on the university's official page, with the link.

[0:55] Benefits

So students stop guessing. They apply only where they can get in and can afford, they don't waste application fees, and their parents see the real cost before anyone commits.

[1:10] Competition

Why not just use ChatGPT? It gives confident answers with no sources, and it only answers what you think to ask. We flag what students don't know to ask, like whether the degree is recognised back home. And the student gets a spreadsheet and a PDF they can show their parents.

[1:35] Current status
