# Slide wording: first draft

*For LaunchX Bootcamp 2, 24 October 2026. Drafted 2026-10-12. **Read it and correct anything
that is not true.** I build the slides only after you have done that.*

Follows the 10 slides of `business/Pitch Deck Template.pdf`. For each slide: the words on it,
what you say out loud, and where the evidence is. A blank like `[____]` is something only you
can fill in. `[SURVEY]` is filled on 21 to 22 October from the survey results.

**Format (your answer, 12 October):** 15 minutes, and it is a feedback session, not a contest.
The template is only an example, so you can include whatever you want feedback on.

That changes the aim. You are not defending the idea. You are showing where it stands and
getting their view on the decisions you cannot make alone. A suggested split: **about 8 minutes
talking, about 7 for their feedback.** Check with iLabs whether the 15 minutes includes
questions.

I kept the template's order because the panel knows it. Spend the time on slides 4, 5, 8 and
10. Slides 1, 3, 6, 7 and 9 can be 20 to 30 seconds each.

## Slide 2: the problem

**On the slide**

> Choosing a university is a **RM200,000 to RM400,000 decision** that lasts 3 to 4 years.
> Most families make it using a few famous names and a chatbot.
>
> - Students do not know what to ask, so "just Gemini it" feels like enough.
> - Students worried about money fall back on names they already know.
> - The person who pays, the parent, is not the one doing the research.

**Say:** your one true story, in two or three sentences. Fill this in:

> "[A friend of mine] wanted to [____]. They were worried about [____], so they [____]. What
> they did not know was [____]."

Do not use the friend's real name. If survey question 8 gives you a better story in someone's
own words, use that instead.

**Evidence:**
- RM200,000 to RM400,000 is an **estimate** from the totals in our own research lists. Say
  "from the lists I have built" if asked.
- The three bullets are your observations of 12 October. Until the survey is in, say "what I
  have seen so far", not "students do X".
- `[SURVEY]` top worry (question 6) and what tools people used (question 4).

## Slide 3: the solution

**On the slide**

> **A list you keep, built around you.**
> 1. **The List:** 8 to 12 universities matched to your grades and budget. Total cost of the
>    whole degree in ringgit. Scholarships for each one.
> 2. **The Report:** a deep file on your favourites, including reasons **not** to go.
> 3. **The Apply Guide:** every deadline in one place, step by step.

**Say:** "Gemini answers what you ask. This tells you what to ask." Then open the demo and one
real report PDF. Showing it does more than describing it.

**Then one line on what is next:** "Today it works for Sunway A-level students and starts from a
Google Form. Next: more qualifications, a proper sign-up page, and help with essays, which is
what students tell me they fear most."

**Evidence:** `business/sales/service-tiers.md`, the demo in `prototype/`, and
`business/sales/sample_pack/`.

**Check before the day:** the demo shows ringgit totals from an older exchange rate, and says
"first report free, each one after is paid" with no price. If asked: "these are sample figures".

## Slide 4: market validation (proof people want it)

**On the slide**

> **What I know**
> - 29 students signed up. 15 have received a deep report.
> - 13 rated the list: average 4.1 out of 5. 6 rated the report: average 4.2 out of 5.
> - One student came back and asked for a second report without being asked.
> - They complained about specifics ("didn't see Warwick"). Polite people don't do that.
>
> **What I do not know yet**
> - All 29 are friends, and it was free. Nobody has paid.
>
> **What I did about it**
> - `[SURVEY]` ___ answers, ___ from people I do not know.
> - `[SURVEY]` ___ left a WhatsApp number to buy a list at RM50. ___ of those were strangers.
> - `[SURVEY]` ___ of ___ parents said yes to RM50.

**Say:** "I have proof the product is usable. I do not yet have proof strangers will pay. Here
is how I am finding out, and the pilot test in November is where I get the real answer."

This is the slide the panel presses hardest. Splitting it into known and not known is what makes
the rest of the deck believable.

**Evidence:** feedback totals in `data/feedback/` (private, totals only), progress counts as of
2026-10-12, and [survey.md](survey.md).

## Slide 7: how I reach the first 100 customers

**On the slide**

> 1. **Pilot test, 10 to 13 November:** sell to the people who left a WhatsApp number.
> 2. **Sunway A-level (about 477 a cohort):** word of mouth, with a real sample report to
>    forward.
> 3. **Parents:** the report is a PDF the student forwards. The file does the introducing.
> 4. **Other colleges:** through friends of friends.
>
> No paid ads. At RM50 a sale, an ad would cost more than it earns.

**Say:** "Students choosing a university all talk to each other. One forwarded report is worth
more than an ad, because it arrives from a friend."

**Evidence:** `business/sales/go-to-market.md`. `[SURVEY]` who decides and who pays
(question 7) tells you whether step 3 should move to the top.

## Slide 8: revenue and costs

**On the slide**

> - **Price today:** RM50 for a list. No revenue yet.
> - **My time per student:** under 1 hour of hands-on work (a list plus one report).
> - **Tools:** about RM158 a month in total. Four students a month cover it.
> - **What limits growth is interruptions, not hours.** Each student needs me at my laptop at
>   3 or 4 separate moments, so under an hour of work takes most of a day to finish.
> - **Next:** cut those moments (one sitting a day, then automate checking and sending), and
>   test a higher price with parents.

**Say:** "This is cheap to run, so I am not here for money. At RM50 it is also small: about
RM4,000 a year at my current pace. The price is what I need to test."

**Evidence:** [unit_economics.md](unit_economics.md). Time figures are yours (12 October). Tool
prices are sourced there.

## Slide 10: what I want your feedback on

This is a feedback session, so this slide matters most. End on questions, not on a request.

**On the slide:** two or three of these. **Your decision.** My suggestion is all three.

> 1. **Who is the buyer?** The student at RM50, or the parent at a higher price?
> 2. **What is the front door?** "University research", or "which scholarships can you get and
>    what will it really cost"?
> 3. **What would you need to see from my pilot test (10 to 13 November)** to believe this is
>    worth continuing?

Question 3 is the most useful one. Their answer becomes your target for November, and it turns
"kill, persevere or pivot" into something you can measure.

**Smaller, underneath:** the two things iLabs could actually give you.

> - An introduction to Sunway College's placement office and counsellors.
> - A mentor who has sold to parents or worked in education.

I left funding off. [unit_economics.md](unit_economics.md) shows the tools cost about RM1,900 a
year, so money is not what is holding this back, and saying so makes the other asks stronger.

Plus your contact: email, and a QR code if you have a page to point it at.

**Say:** "Those are the three things I most want your view on."
