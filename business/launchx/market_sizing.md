# Market size: how many customers exist

*For slide 5 (Market Sizing). Researched 2026-10-12. Read this, question it, then tell me what
looks wrong.*

## The three numbers

TAM, SAM and SOM are three slices, each smaller than the one before. Each is "number of students
× what one pays". The only price that exists today is RM50 for a list, so that is what I used.

| Slice | Who it is | Students per year | × RM50 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TAM** (total addressable market: everyone who could ever want this) | Every Malaysian school-leaver deciding what to do next | **366,435** | **about RM18.3 million a year** |
| **SAM** (serviceable addressable market: the part the product fits today) | Malaysians who start a degree abroad each year | **about 15,500** (estimate) | **about RM775,000 a year** |
| **SOM** (serviceable obtainable market: who you can realistically reach in 1 to 2 years) | Sunway College A-level students, two cohorts | **about 950** (477 a cohort) | **about RM47,700 over two years** |

## Where each number comes from

**TAM: 366,435.** The number of candidates who qualified for the SPM (Sijil Pelajaran Malaysia,
the national school-leaving exam) certificate in 2025.
Source: [New Straits Times, 31 March 2026](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/news/nation/2026/03/1407188/spm-2025-record-366435-candidates-qualify-certificate).
This is a ceiling, not a forecast. Many of these students do not go on to a degree.

**SAM: about 15,500 a year. This one is an estimate.** How I got it:

1. A news report gives 54,440 Malaysians studying abroad in 2022 (and 56,253 in 2021).
   Source: [The Sun, September 2023](https://thesun.my/malaysia-news/brain-drain-major-loss-of-human-capital-for-country-minister-BB11543839).
2. That is everyone abroad at once, across all years of study. To get the number who **start**
   each year I divided by 3.5, a typical degree length. 54,440 ÷ 3.5 = about 15,500.

The division by 3.5 is my assumption. Two other sources put the total in the same range, which
is why I trust the size if not the exact figure: UNESCO (the United Nations education body) gives
59,144, quoted in a
[US Department of Commerce guide](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/malaysia-education-and-training),
and a 2024 industry report gives "over 50,000", quoted by
[The PIE News, 11 August 2025](https://thepienews.com/international-students-flock-to-malaysia-as-demand-surges/).

I chose "students who go abroad" for SAM because that is where the product is clearly useful:
several countries compared, total cost in ringgit, and whether the degree is recognised back
home.

**SOM: 477 a cohort.** The number of Sunway College students who sat the Cambridge A-level exams
in May/June 2026.
Source: [The Star, 9 September 2026](https://www.thestar.com.my/starpicks/2026/09/09/to-a-level-and-beyond-at-sunway-college)
(a sponsored article, so the number comes from Sunway itself).
For scale: your 29 testers are about 6% of one cohort, if they were all Sunway A-level students.
Please confirm that.

## What the numbers say

**At RM50, the market you can reach is small, and the panel will notice.** RM775,000 a year
is the whole serviceable market, before any competitor takes a share.

This does not say the idea is weak. It says the lever is the **price**, not the number of
students. Three things point the same way:

- Families who pay for help at all pay far more than RM50. One Malaysian agency's paid packages
  start at RM2,000 ([Inquota](https://inquota.com.my/)). See [competitors.md](competitors.md).
- Your own observation: the student does not feel the risk. The parent, who pays RM200k to
  RM400k, does.
- The product review already suggested a cheap student entry plus a parent-priced full package.

Here is the same SAM at other prices. **This is an illustration, not a price I am proposing.**
What a parent would actually pay is unknown, and it is exactly what the parent chats in
[survey.md](survey.md) test.

| Price per student | SAM (about 15,500 students a year) |
|---|---|
| RM50 | about RM775,000 |
| RM150 | about RM2.3 million |
| RM300 | about RM4.7 million |

So the honest line for the slide is: *"At RM50 this is a small business. The open question is
what a parent would pay, and I am testing that now."* A panel hears that as someone who has done
the sums.

## What I left out, and why

- **Students choosing between local private universities.** About 527,000 Malaysians are
  enrolled in private institutions (my subtraction from figures the Ministry of Higher Education
  gave in [The Star, 5 July 2026](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/07/05/international-students-account-for-126-of-higher-education-institutions039-enrollment-says-ministry):
  1,264,541 total minus 634,706 in public universities, minus 102,573 international students in
  private ones). That is a much bigger group, but the product has not been tested on a student
  who is only choosing between local options. If asked, call it a possible later market.
- **Students who consider going abroad and then stay.** They would use the product too, so the
  real SAM is larger than 15,500. Nobody publishes that number. The counsellor chat (question 4
  in survey.md) is the cheapest way to get a feel for it.
