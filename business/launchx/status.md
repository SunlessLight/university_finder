## Where things stand

The product works: 29 friends used it free. Survey (out 12 Oct): 79 responses (12 strangers, 25 "a
little", 42 friends); figures in [survey.md](survey.md), CSV in `data/form/` (gitignored). **29 real
leads at RM50, all messaged by 23 Oct; 4 have paid a deposit** (Evan, 24 Oct; spreadsheet not yet
updated). Families are the main track; consultancies are learning chats. Evan is in exams.

## Decided

- **Focus (24 Oct):** families first, with Evan learning marketing and referrals. Consultancies =
  learning chats only, no selling. Backend: cost cuts measured on the 2 Nov lists, then only fixes
  a customer would see. Internal fixes in additionals.md stay parked.
- **Continue-bar (24 Oct): 20 paid family lists by 2 Dec.** On a miss: pause, work out why,
  decide continue / pivot / stop by 10 Dec.
- **Prices (23 Oct):** list (20–30 universities) RM50, single report RM30. Count the 25 Oct
  replies at the old price, then announce RM30 in the nudge.
- **Pre-sale cap: 20 list spots** (24 Oct, up from 15), set by Evan's time (about 20 hours).
- **After payment** the customer gets the existing Google Form, not WhatsApp.
- **Referral reward:** a free one-university report per friend who pays; only for people who have
  paid and received their list.
- **Firecrawl costed** at its paid price (about RM77 a month). No legal or tax setup until a
  business pays (SSM registration then).
