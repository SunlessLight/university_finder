# 02. Longlist instructions (Stage 3: how helpers research each university row)

*Fact audit, 2026-10-28. Written for Evan (no technical background). Labels: G guess, A advice,
Y yapping, J unlabelled judgment, L loophole. Severity: High reaches a student, Medium could,
Low internal.*

## What this part makes
These are the written instructions that tell the helpers (the "row-filler", a separate AI sent off
to research one university at a time) how to fill each row of the student's spreadsheet (the
`master_list.csv`, the "longlist"). Think of them as the recipe card each helper follows. They
include the main how-to (`03_discover_longlist.md`), the field-by-field rulebook
(`03b_candidate_schema.md`), a list of good websites (`sources.md`), one "tips and traps" sheet per
country (`workflows/countries/*.md`), the helper's own job description (`row-filler.md`) and the
`/longlist` launcher. The recipes do not reach the student themselves, but every value, hedge or
piece of advice they *invite* ends up in the spreadsheet cells (`Notes`, `Post-study work`,
`Recognition`, `Key deadline`…) or in the student's long-form `research_notes.md`.

## Who sees it
Helpers and Evan read these files. The student sees what the helpers write because of them: the
longlist spreadsheet (Google Sheets) and `research_notes.md` in their folder.

## What is already solid
- **"Aggregators find, official pages prove."** Stated plainly in 03:112-113, row-filler.md:124-128 and sources.md:3-6, with a reason: every number in a cell must come from the university's or government's own page.
- **"Never invent a fact to fill a cell"** (row-filler.md:136-143), with a fixed list of honest "no answer" values in code (`SENTINEL_VALUES`) instead of blanks or guesses.
- **The English-waiver rule** (03:318-323, row-filler.md:129-135) was rewritten on 2026-10-28 after the "likely waived" errors: waivers only in the university's own words.
- **`entry_margin` means grades only** (03b:102-121), with the reason and the real bug it fixed. Only the Japan and USA files contradict it (finding 14).
- **The UK living-cost rule** (uk.md:33-36): with no university figure, use the gov.uk visa figure on the day and say so in Notes. It is a labelled stand-in, not a hidden guess.
- **China's deadline rule** (china.md:112-116) and Taiwan's aggregator-policy warning (taiwan.md:111-114): use the "not published" value instead of last year's date, and confirm an aggregator claim on the official page before it goes in a row.
