# 09. Promises vs reality

*Fact audit, 2026-10-28. Written for Evan (no technical background). Labels: G guess, A advice,
Y yapping, J unlabelled judgment, L loophole. Severity: High reaches a student, Medium could,
Low internal.*

## What this part makes

This part is not a program. It is the set of **promises** the business writes down: the five
"product report" explainers (`business/product_report/*.md`), the sales script
(`business/sales/positioning.md`) and the sample-pack note (`business/sales/sample_pack/README.md`).
They tell customers and investors things like "every fact is read from the university's own
website". This audit compares each promise with what actually stops a mistake. There are three
kinds of protection:
- **A check (a script that fails).** A small program reads the file and refuses to pass it, like a
  spell-checker that blocks sending. `check_master_list.py` checks the spreadsheet;
  `check_report.py` checks a university report. This is the strong kind.
- **An instruction.** A written rule the AI helper is told to follow. The AI can ignore it or get
  it wrong, and nothing notices.
- **Nothing.** In that case the promise is only words.

Note: the sample-pack files were being edited by another session during this audit (uncommitted
changes). The quotes from `sample_master_list.csv` are from the working copy on 2026-10-28.

## Who sees it

- **The public / prospects:** the sales script is what Evan says out loud, and the sample pack
  (`sample_master_list.csv`, `sample_glossary.csv`, the MIT report PDF, the US apply-guide PDF) is
  sent to prospects and shown at the booth.
- **Investors / LaunchX:** the product reports explain the product. They could be shared.
- **Students:** they get the spreadsheet, reports, recommendation and calendar the promises describe.

## What is already solid

- **Fit and chance of entry really are kept apart in code.** `validate_weights()` refuses
  entry-fit weight keys (`tools/shortlist_schema.py:249`, `:268`). `check_master_list.py` fails a
  "Grades vs entry bar" that contradicts "Admission likelihood". The sales claim at
  positioning.md:59-60 holds (only its line number, `:113`, is out of date).
- **Gaps can't be hidden by an empty cell.** The completeness check fails blanks, allows the
  approved non-answers ("Rolling", "Not ranked", "Not published") only in their own columns, and
  the cost range check catches broken totals.
- **Half-finished documents can't be built.** `build_report.py` refuses empty sections or no
  sources. `build_application_prep.py:326-344` refuses a guide missing deadlines, checklist,
  financial aid or sources, and its message says to write "Not found — <why>" instead.
- **check_report.py holds the report to evidence rules.** Each "Not found" needs a reason, there is
  a cap of 8, and each source needs a link, an official-or-not label and a year.
- **The calendar never guesses a date.** Vague dates go to "to confirm" with the university's own
  wording (decide_report.md:202-205).
- **The documents are honest about their limits.** Every product report has an "Honest limits"
  section. longlist_report.md:18 calls the score and Reach/Match/Safety "two separate judgements",
  "Approx total (MYR)" is labelled an estimate (line 240), and `qa_drill.md:81` forbids saying
  "every fact is verified".
