Low internal.*

## What this part makes
These are the scripts (small Python programs) that turn the research helpers' notes on each
university into rows of the student's spreadsheet (`master_list.csv`, the "master list"). They
work out five cells nobody types by hand: the 0–100 **Desirability** score and its **Tier**
(A/B/C), **Admission likelihood** (Reach / Match / Safety), **Grades vs entry bar** ("Well
above" … "Well below" / "Not published"), **Warnings** (fixed short phrases like "Over budget")
and **Approx total (MYR)** (whole-degree cost converted to ringgit with a fixed exchange-rate
table). `merge_candidates.py` is the "is every box filled?" gate before rows are added;
`sync_shortlist.py` adds them; `apply_backfill.py` patches holes in rows that already exist;
`check_master_list.py` is the final inspection ("lint", like a spell-checker for the
spreadsheet) before a list goes to a student. `firecrawl_search.py` is the paid web-search
helper the researchers use. The `scoring-weights` skill explains how each student's priority
ranking becomes the score's weights (how much each factor counts).

## Who sees it
- **Student:** every cell these scripts write lands in `master_list.csv`, which the student
  receives (opened in Google Sheets / Excel).
- **The public:** the same cells appear in `business/sales/sample_pack/sample_master_list.csv`
  and the demo website (`prototype/data.js`), built from one real list.
- **Evan / helpers only:** console messages, `score_log.jsonl`, `weights.json`, search results.

## What is already solid
- **No silent default weights.** `compute_score` has no fallback weights, `load_weights` refuses a
  missing file or blank rationale, and `validate_weights` rejects admissibility keys, weights over
  0.5 and sums that aren't 1.0. A student is never scored under weights nobody chose.
- **Desirability is kept apart from "can I get in".** `FORBIDDEN_WEIGHT_KEYS` stops entry fit from
  raising a score, and the Reach/Match/Safety and Warnings columns sit beside it.
- **Computed cells can't be hand-typed by a backfill.** `apply_backfill.py` refuses
  `COMPUTED_COLUMNS`, unknown columns, over-length cells and overwrites without `--overwrite`, and
  writes nothing unless every fragment passes.
- **Honest non-answers exist.** `SENTINEL_VALUES` ("Not ranked", "No statistics published", "Not
  published" for grades) plus `grade_fit_label`'s `not_published` path let a helper say "no such
  fact" instead of inventing one. The merge and backfill failure messages say "never a guess".
- **Safe defaults.** A missing `source_authority` becomes "Not verified" (sync_shortlist.py:149),
  not "Official page". A messy budget gives "no ceiling", never a fake RM 1. A missing grade margin
  leaves the cell blank and the completeness check flags it.
- **Blank Course URL is caught.** Course URL is a required column, so an "Official page" row with no
  link fails `check_completeness`. Out-of-range ringgit totals fail `check_plausibility`.
