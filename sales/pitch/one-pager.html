What you actually get — three parts
Tier 1 — The List

The shortlist

Your real options, costed in ringgit.
  • 20–40 uni + course matches to your grades, budget & target countries — then you pick 3–5 to go deep on
  • One spreadsheet, 35 columns per uni, opens in Google Sheets
  • Two scores kept separate: a 0–100 quality score vs Reach / Match / Safety — a uni you can’t get into never ranks #1
  • Total cost of the whole degree in MYR — not per year, so a 3-yr UK and 4-yr US degree compare fairly
  • Recognised in Malaysia? flagged (MQA + professional body) — the gate for regulated careers
  • Every row stamped verified / not-yet, so you know which numbers are actually checked
Tier 2 — The Dossiers

The deep dive

Can I get in, will I belong, what will it take?
  • A deep PDF report for each uni you shortlisted — built one university at a time
  • 16 sections each, with all the information you need
  • “Who actually gets in” — real admitted-student grades, activities & essay angles (offer-holder threads, US Common Data Set, NUS cutoffs)
  • Full cost + scholarships — government and company sponsors (JPA / MARA / Yayasan and Petronas, Shell, Khazanah, Bank Negara…), visa & post-study work rights
  • “Will I belong” — study intensity, student vibe, city feel, plus the halal / prayer / Malaysian- community angle
  • Honest “why hesitate” on every uni — where it’s weak for you, checked against the official page first
Tier 3 — The Apply Pack

The how-to-apply

Now go do it — nothing missed.
  • A ranked recommendation of your finalists + a realistic apply strategy (UCAS’s 5-choice cap, Oxbridge either/or, app-fee budget)
  • One deadline calendar across every country — applications, scholarships, tests, visa lead times, in order
  • A per-region apply guide (PDF) — US / UK / Australia / Singapore / China, grouped by system so shared work is done once
  • Per uni: deadlines (incl. EA/ED + the separate aid deadline), tests, essays, fees + fee waivers, checklist, portal links
  • Real financial aid — what it covers and what you still pay (flights, visa, the student contribution “100% of need” hides), in USD + approx MYR
