What you actually get
01 — The List

Your real options

Not just famous names — the ones that fit you.
  • 8-12 universities and course per country
  • Every fee, requirement and deadline checked on the actual university page
  • Sorted into "you're probably in," "it's a stretch," "you're basically guaranteed" — so at least you know what you're dealing with.
  • Full cost of the whole degree, in ringgit — Includes tuition cost, living expenses, etc
  • Highlights whether the degree even counts back home for medicine, engineering, law, and other licensed careers
  • One spreadsheet you keep, opens in Google Sheets — with a plain-English glossary tab for every acronym
02 — The Report

Your top picks, deep

Can I get in, will I like it, what will it take?
  • A full PDF for each uni you pick — an actual document you can read to decide
  • Who actually gets in — real grades, activities and essay angles from people who got offers
  • What it's actually like day to day — workload, the vibe, the community.
  • Full cost, plus every scholarship worth applying for — government and company sponsors
  • Visa, work rights after you graduate, whether it's recognised back home
  • The part most people skip: every report says why NOT to go. A list that only says nice things isn't one you can trust.
03 — The Apply Guide

Now go do it

Exactly what to do, in order, nothing missed.
  • Every deadline, test, essay and fee for each uni you're applying to
  • Grouped so shared work only happens once — not redoing the same steps six times
  • The real financial-aid math: what's actually covered vs. what you still pay out of pocket
  • One calendar, every deadline, every country, so nothing sneaks past you
  • One region at a time (US, UK, Australia, Singapore, China) — built when you're ready for it
